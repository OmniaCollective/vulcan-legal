## Description of Service

Vulcan is a mobile application designed for tracking custom motorcycle build projects. The App allows you to:

- Create and manage build projects for custom motorcycles
- Track costs including parts and labour
- Upload and organize build progress photos
- Use voice-to-text input for quick data entry
- Generate customer portal preview links
- Access a directory of motorcycle builders (mock/demonstration data)
- Manage build schedules with start dates and deadlines

## Voice Input and AI Features

### How Voice Input Works

When you use the voice input feature:
1. You hold the microphone button and speak
2. Audio is recorded on your device
3. Audio is sent to **Groq's Whisper API** for transcription
4. Transcribed text is returned to the App
5. **Audio is immediately deleted** - not stored on our servers or your device

### Important Limitations

**Accuracy Not Guaranteed**
- Transcription accuracy depends on audio quality, accent, background noise
- You are responsible for reviewing transcribed text for accuracy
- We are not liable for transcription errors

**Language Support**
- Primarily supports English
- Other languages may work but with reduced accuracy

**Data Processing**
- Voice data processed by Groq (US-based servers)
- Subject to Groq's terms and privacy policy
- See Privacy Policy for full details

**You Agree To:**
- Review transcribed text before saving

## Customer Portals

### Portal Feature Explanation

Solo Builder and Pro Shop subscribers can publish a **real, hosted
web page** for a customer at a link like
`builtbyomnia.com/vulcan-build/{id}`, which:
- Requires no app, account, or login for the customer to view
- Shows a snapshot of build progress, photos, cost total, and
  deadline — you control which of these are visible via toggles in the
  app
- Is a **point-in-time snapshot**, not a live view — the customer sees
  whatever you last published, until you publish an update
- Is accessible to **anyone who has the link**; there is currently no
  password, expiry, or automatic removal, so treat the link like you
  would any unlisted but unprotected web page

**Your Responsibility:**
- Only share the link with the customer it's intended for
- Keep the visibility toggles set appropriately for what you're
  comfortable sharing (e.g. turn off cost visibility if you don't want
  the customer to see it)
- Delete or stop sharing a portal (via the app) once it's no longer
  needed
